Privacy policy - Christine Sable

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Christine Sable is a personal tool used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer, with the developer's own Google account.

What data is accessed

With the developer's permission, the app accesses Gmail messages in that one account (Gmail "modify" access) so it can read incoming mail and apply labels.

How it is used

Email content is used only to sort and flag messages for the developer. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with any other person or company.

Storage and sharing

The app's sign-in token is stored on the developer's own computer. Email content is processed on that computer and is not copied to any server run by the developer. Parts of a message may be processed by an AI assistant service the developer uses to summarise and sort it, under that service's own terms.

Retention and deletion

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops the app from reading mail.
